## Coverage and contents

The narrative opens with Italian geography and the end of the separate Western Roman emperorship in AD 476. It covers medieval and early modern Italy, foreign rule, the Risorgimento, and national unification in 1870. Its final section is "Italy since 1870"; the last narrative page (printed p. 260) explicitly describes the situation as "now (1874)."

This is an older school history, not a modern comprehensive survey. Ancient Italy before AD 476 is only background. It does not cover the late nineteenth century in full, fascism, either world war, the republic, or contemporary Italy.

The contents and corresponding chapter headings in the full OCR file include all eleven chapters:

1. The Emperor and the Barbarians
2. The Emperor and the Pope
3. The Emperor and the Cities
4. The Greatness of the Guelfs
5. The Ghibelin Lords
6. The Great States
7. Italy Invaded
8. Italy Conquered
9. Italy Enslaved
10. Italy Divided
11. Italy Free and United

The chronology ends at 1870; the prose continues with the short contemporary assessment through 1874. These are different endpoints and should not be conflated.
